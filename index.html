Darren Doyle

Trinidadian-born writer currently based in Cambridgeshire, UK.

About

Darren Doyle's work was shortlisted for the Commonwealth Short Story Prize and has appeared in Susumba's Book Bag. His fiction explores the complexities of Caribbean identity, perception, and history.
