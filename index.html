About

Darren Doyle is a writer and editor based in Ireland. His work has appeared in literary journals, magazines, and anthologies across Ireland, the UK, and internationally. He is interested in the essay form, short fiction, and the ways language shapes how we understand place and memory.


He has worked as an editor and collaborator on several independent publishing projects and is available for commissions, editorial work, and writing residencies.
